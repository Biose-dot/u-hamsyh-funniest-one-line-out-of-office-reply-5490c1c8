Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8) competition **"Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply"** ([bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8)).

One line, 135 characters: an out-of-office reply whose auto-reply has taken over the inbox, promoted itself to manager, and is now ignoring your email on the sender's behalf — a fresh turn on the usual away message, with the laugh landing on the first read.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) copies `entry.txt` byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it:

> Out of office until Monday — my auto-reply took over my inbox, promoted itself to manager, and is now ignoring your email on my behalf.

The text is 135 characters (UTF-8, within the 1–160 limit), a single line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
